Random Name Picker Wheel

Paste your list, press spin, and let the wheel decide. Fair, random, and it keeps working without internet.

Commas work as separators too. Names are saved on this device automatically.

Tip: switch on “Remove winner” to call on several students without repeats.

How to use the name picker wheel

  1. Paste your student list into the box above — one name per line (commas work too).
  2. Press Spin the wheel. It slows down and stops on one name at random; the winner appears under the wheel.
  3. Press Share result to send a picture of the winner to your class group chat, or keep spinning.

What teachers use it for

  • Calling on a student to answer or present without looking like you are picking favourites.
  • Deciding the order of oral exams, reading aloud or lab rotations.
  • Raffling small prizes, clean-up duties or group leaders.
  • Filling the last two minutes of a lesson with one quick spin that gets everyone looking up.

Why every name has the same chance

Each name gets an equal slice of the wheel, and the winning slice is chosen with the browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues) rather than Math.random. The list is reshuffled every time the wheel is drawn, so a name that happened to sit at the top of your list does not start with better odds.

If you leave “Remove winner” switched on, a picked name is taken out of the pool before the next spin. That turns the same wheel into a no-repeat draw for the whole class — the useful setting for presentations, where nobody should be called twice.

No sign-up, nothing uploaded

The list is stored in your own browser's local storage and is never sent to a server. Once the page has loaded it also runs offline: close the laptop lid, lose the school Wi-Fi, and the wheel still spins because everything happens inside the page.

It scales from a phone held up in front of the class to a projector at the back of the room — the wheel redraws to fit the screen and the winner text stays large enough to read from the last row.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a random name with this wheel?

Paste your list of names (one per line) into the box, press Spin, and the wheel stops on a single name. The winner is shown under the wheel and can be copied or shared.

How many names can I add?

There is no hard limit. Up to 20 names the labels stay on two lines; past 20 the labels are shortened automatically so the wheel remains readable.

Is the spin really random and fair?

Yes. The stopping angle comes from the browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues), not from a predictable number, and the names are shuffled again before the wheel is drawn.

Are my student names stored on a server?

No. The list is saved only in your own browser's local storage and is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I use the wheel without internet?

Yes. Once the page has been opened once, it runs from the browser's offline cache, so a dropped connection in class does not stop the spin.
