Frequently asked questions

Is LessonKit really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use without an account, without usage limits and without intrusive ads.

Is my student list sent to a server?

No. The names you paste are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.

Can I use the tools without internet?

Yes. Once a page has been opened it is cached by the browser and keeps working when the classroom Wi-Fi drops.
