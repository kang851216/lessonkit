Ways to use a timer in class

  • 5 minutes — group discussion before presentations.
  • 10 minutes — a short quiz or a set of practice questions.
  • 1 minute — a quick-fire round or a warm-up.
  • 15 minutes — independent work, writing or silent reading.
  • Guide: a 30-minute exam timer

Why a visible timer helps

A deadline everyone can see keeps students focused and makes it easier for the teacher to hold the pace of the lesson. Large digits also cut down the "how many minutes left?" questions.

This timer runs entirely in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and it keeps working offline once the page has been opened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this class timer?

Pick a preset (1, 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes) or press +1 min, then press Start. The number counts down in a large display that is visible from the back of the room.

Does it make a sound when time is up?

Yes. The timer beeps three times and the digits turn red for the last 10 seconds.

Does the screen stay awake while the timer runs?

Yes, in browsers that support it the screen is kept awake, so the projector does not drop to a blank screen.

Can the timer be used without internet?

Yes. It runs entirely in the browser and keeps working when the connection drops.
