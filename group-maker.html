Random Group Maker

Paste your list, pick how many groups you want, and deal the class out at random. Built for group work, discussion and class projects.

Names are saved on this device automatically and shared with the name picker wheel.

When to use a random group maker

  • Everyday group work, so students do not keep choosing the same partners.
  • A group discussion that appears at the end of the lesson and needs groups in ten seconds.
  • Teams for a quiz, a project, or a class competition across several lessons.
  • Rotating groups each week so everyone gets to work with everyone.
  • Guide: splitting a class into groups of four

How it works

  1. Paste the list of names (one per line).
  2. Choose the number of groups and the kind of roles.
  3. Press Make groups. The list is shuffled and then dealt out evenly.
  4. Save the result as an image to post in the class chat.

Group sizes are not always identical

When the class does not divide evenly, the leftover students go into the first groups, one each. For example 23 students in 4 groups gives 6, 6, 6 and 5. Nobody is left without a group.

Fair, and not up to the teacher's mood

The shuffle uses Fisher-Yates with the browser's cryptographic random source and runs twice — once for the order of the names, once for the order of the roles. Everything happens on your device, and the list is never sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I split students into random groups?

Paste the list of names, choose the number of groups (2–10), then press Make groups. Every student lands in exactly one group and the group sizes are kept as even as possible.

Is the split actually fair?

Yes. The names are shuffled first and then dealt out in turn, so the biggest group has at most one member more than the smallest.

Can I save the groups?

Yes. Download the result as a PNG image to post in the class chat, or as a CSV file to open in Excel.

Is student data safe?

Yes. Everything runs in the browser; no name is ever sent to a server.
